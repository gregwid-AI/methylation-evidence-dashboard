Three executive takeaways

The decision-relevant view, with a clear boundary between what the reports propose and what external sources establish.

01 / MEDICATION SAFETY

Reconcile the regimen before adding to it.

The dossier raises interaction concerns around brexpiprazole and fluvoxamine, plus lithium monitoring. Exact dose decisions depend on indication, inhibitor strength, patient factors, and the current label; the report's fixed-dose rules should not be treated as prescriptions.

02 / EVIDENCE BOUNDARIES

Genetics is context, not a complete explanation.

The source attributes symptoms to MTHFR, MTR/MTRR, and COMT. These reports do not establish that the variants caused this individual's symptoms. CPIC does not support using SLC6A4 results for antidepressant prescribing; AACAP cautions against selecting psychotropics through pharmacogenetic tests in youth.

R03 / CPIC guideline → R05 / Youth guidance →
03 / OUTCOMES & MONITORING

Proposals are not observed improvements.

The source's laboratory targets, weekly timelines, and remission percentages are proposed or asserted, not this patient's measured results. Adult depression trials and a small adolescent NAC pilot do not validate the entire combined protocol or guarantee symptom resolution.

The biochemical model in the reports

A navigation map of the proposed mechanism, not proof of deficiency, individual brain chemistry, or a treatment indication.

01   FOLATE PROCESSING →

MTHFR / Folate cycle

The reports propose reduced active-folate availability and methylfolate support. Common MTHFR variants do not mean folic acid cannot be processed or must be avoided.

R06 / CDC clarification

02   ONE-CARBON METABOLISM →

B12 / Methionine / SAMe

Vitamin B12 has an established role in methionine synthase and one-carbon metabolism. The reports' claims of individual SAMe depletion and melatonin failure are not demonstrated by supplied laboratory data.

R07 / NIH B12 fact sheet

03   CATECHOLAMINE METABOLISM

COMT / Dopamine

The reports link COMT Val/Val to dopamine clearance and several symptoms. The claimed 300–400% individual dopamine-clearance rate and symptom-specific causal conclusions are not validated here.
