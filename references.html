Reference library / Traceable evidence

Follow the source. Know the limits.

Linked primary research and authoritative guidance, with a claim-by-claim audit of the supplied reports. Checked November 10, 2026.

What “verified” means here: bibliographic details and the stated evidence boundaries were checked against the linked abstract, guideline, fact sheet, or prescribing information. This is not a systematic review, an independent confirmation of the patient's records, or proof that the combined protocol is effective.

R01Adult randomized trials

L-methylfolate augmentation in SSRI-resistant major depression

Papakostas GI, et al. American Journal of Psychiatry. 2012;169(12):1267–1274. DOI: 10.1176/appi.ajp.2012.11071114. PMID: 23212058.

Referencable data
Two 60-day studies: 148 participants in the first, 75 in the second. The first found no significant between-group outcome difference. The second, using 15 mg/day, favored adjunctive methylfolate; number needed to treat for response was approximately six.
What it does not establish
It does not validate this adolescent's combined regimen, genotype-based causal narrative, OCD or self-injury treatment, or guaranteed remission. The dose is a study description, not a recommendation.

R02Preliminary / Open-label

N-acetylcysteine for nonsuicidal self-injury in adolescents

Cullen KR, et al. Journal of Child and Adolescent Psychopharmacology. 2018;28(2):136–144. DOI: 10.1089/cap.2017.0032. PMID: 29053023.

Referencable data
Single-arm, open-label pilot: 35 female participants aged 13–21; 24 completed. NSSI frequency declined at later visits. The authors call for a randomized, placebo-controlled trial.
Correction to the source report
The matching adolescent NSSI paper is by Cullen and colleagues, not the report's “Grant et al., 2018” citation. It is not double-blind and does not prove efficacy or self-harm cessation.

R03CPIC guideline

Genotypes and serotonin reuptake inhibitor antidepressants

Bousman CA, et al. Clinical Pharmacology & Therapeutics. 2023;114(1):51–68. DOI: 10.1002/cpt.2903.

Referencable data
Provides gene/drug-specific prescribing guidance involving CYP2D6, CYP2C19, and CYP2B6. Existing SLC6A4 and HTR2A data do not support their clinical use in antidepressant prescribing.
Application boundary
Interpret the exact genotype, medication, phenotype, and clinical context. This is not support for a universal methylation supplement protocol or an individual “dopamine starvation” diagnosis.
Free full guidelineDOI
R04Prescribing information

Rexulti (brexpiprazole): current U.S. prescribing information

Otsuka / Lundbeck. Manufacturer-hosted U.S. prescribing label; checked November 10, 2026.

Referencable data
Section 2.7 and Table 1 describe CYP-related dosage modifications, including combinations of CYP2D6 and CYP3A4 inhibitors. Requirements depend on the inhibitor combination and indication; MDD-specific caveats apply.
Correction to the source report
The dossier's blanket “reduce by ~50%” and fixed 0.5–1 mg statements are not a complete label-based rule. This page does not determine the appropriate dose for the reported combination. The label also states Rexulti is not approved in pediatric patients with MDD.
Official prescribing information (PDF)
R05Adolescent guidance

Clinical use of pharmacogenetic tests in children and adolescents

American Academy of Child and Adolescent Psychiatry (AACAP). Policy statement, 2020.

Referencable guidance
AACAP recommends clinicians avoid using pharmacogenetic testing to select psychotropic medications in children and adolescents and calls for higher-quality prospective studies.
Why it matters here
The reports describe an adolescent and infer broad treatment choices from genetic findings. This policy adds essential context; it does not eliminate all gene/drug-specific pharmacokinetic considerations.
Read the policy
R06CDC / Contrary evidence

MTHFR gene variant and folic acid facts

Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. Updated July 16, 2026.

Referencable data
People with common MTHFR variants can process all forms of folate, including folic acid. At equal folic acid intake, the 677 TT genotype has about 16% lower average blood folate than CC. This is not the same as the source's CT enzyme-activity claim.
Correction to the source report
CDC explicitly says common MTHFR variants are not a reason to avoid folic acid. The report's blanket avoidance directive and claimed 57% brain-transport blockade are not established by this source.
Read the CDC fact sheet
R07NIH / Nutrient reference

Vitamin B12: health professional fact sheet

National Institutes of Health, Office of Dietary Supplements. Accessed November 10, 2026.

Referencable data
B12 is a cofactor for methionine synthase and methylmalonyl-CoA mutase. Methylmalonic acid can help assess B12 status but is also influenced by renal function. Cyanocobalamin and hydroxocobalamin are converted to active forms.
Correction to the source report
Does not support a blanket “cyanide toxicity” warning against cyanocobalamin or prove individual SAMe depletion. Laboratory cutoffs depend on clinical context and the method used.
Read the NIH fact sheet
